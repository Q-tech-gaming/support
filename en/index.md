## Frequently asked questions

### Q1. The app will not start

Restart your device and try again. If the issue continues, contact us with your device name, OS version, and a description of what happens.

### Q2. Can I use this app offline?

Many features, such as Challenge mode and standard matches, work offline.  
An internet connection is required for Time Attack start, result submission, and rankings; VS Quantum AI rankings; and server-side player name registration.

### Q3. Do you collect user information?

You do not need to register a name, email address, or similar personal details. For ranking features, an anonymous identifier, display name, and scores / win counts may be stored on Firebase. See the privacy policy for details.

### Q4. How do I delete my ranking data?

Open **Help & info** from the top-right of the home screen, then choose **Delete ranking data**. This removes Time Attack records, VS Quantum AI win counts, your player name, and other ranking-related data from the server and the device. Challenge progress is not removed.

If you cannot delete the data from the app, contact the address listed in the privacy policy and include the player name shown on the ranking.
